# Selection and scope evidence

Checked 17 October 2026. Family: customer-survey-operations. No build in this registry covered the internal survey response-review desk. Product feedback voting is a different workflow.

Salesforce Feedback Management Starter lists US$13,500 per organisation per month, billed annually: US$162,000 a year before the selected Service Cloud edition. Growth lists US$46,000 per organisation per month. These are public list prices, not a verified NZ or AU customer's invoice. [Vendor pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/service/customer-service-operations/feedback-management/).

Scores in order: annual bill 5, data-shaped 4, our buyers 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 23/30. Buyer fit is provisional: no named customer's purchase of Feedback Management was verified. Search results show several alternatives and pricing comparisons, but no search-volume measurement was available. The score reflects visible intent, not a volume claim.

Qualtrics scored 1/4/2/4/4/5 = 20. Its [pricing page](https://www.qualtrics.com/pricing/) routes enterprise products to a quote, so no annual figure was assumed. AskNicely scored 1/4/3/3/4/5 = 20. Its [pricing page](https://www.asknicely.com/pricing) also requires a scoped quote. Neither qualified ahead of Salesforce's explicit list price. Docebo was excluded because learning software overlaps Enterprise DNA's business.

[Salesforce export documentation](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.task_export_responses.htm&language=en_US&type=5) describes CSV response export, with question headers and possible extra rows for ranking or multiple-selection items. The importer supports explicitly mapped numeric and text questions only. No unsupported export is described as a direct import.

An operator's rituals here are response triage, segment score review, recovery follow-up, evidence-backed closure and a weekly record review. The implementation has no hosted survey builder. No claim is made that Salesforce cannot answer these questions with custom reports. The ten questions in the README are executed against this build's own data.
